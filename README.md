# Nsangeet — नेपाली संगीतको रेडियो

A cozy online radio for Nepali music. Tune in and listen to a hand-picked
playlist of hit songs from Nepal's most-loved artists — all in one place,
with synced karaoke lyrics that follow the singer in real time.

## What you get

- **Always-on radio vibes** — pick an artist and let the songs flow one
  after another, like a real FM station (अनुश्रुत FM).
- **Karaoke-style lyrics** — the current line lights up and scrolls in
  sync with the music, the way Spotify does, for 45 songs across 9 artists.
- **Artist switcher** — jump between artists from the top-right menu.
- **Full playback control** — play/pause, skip tracks, and drag the
  progress bar to any point in the song.
- **Works like an app** — installable on your phone or desktop (PWA), so
  Nsangeet lives on your home screen and starts offline.
- **Beautiful at any size** — a clean, calm layout that suits both desktop
  and mobile screens.

## Artists on the radio

Sabin Rai · John Rai · Bartika Eam Rai · Ankita Pun · Sajjan Raj Vaidya ·
Sushant KC · Samir Shrestha · The Elements · The Tribal Rain

## Start listening

Open the site, choose an artist, and press play — that's it. The station
takes care of the rest, moving from song to song with synced lyrics along
the way.
